# OP2: whether research focuses on health

---

Some people think scientific research should focus on solving world health problems. Others think that there are more important issues. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

---

**Model answer**: 

**Opinions differ over whether** scientific research should primarily focus on global health problems or give greater attention to other major challenges. Although medical research is **essential for protecting human life and improving well-being**, I believe environmental and technological research can sometimes **deserve equal or even greater priority** because their effects **extend across many areas of society**.

**On the one hand**, research into health has **clear and immediate benefits**. Medical scientists **play a crucial role in responding to global health emergencies**, particularly when new diseases emerge. During the COVID-19 pandemic, for example, rapid scientific research **contributed to the development of effective vaccines and treatments**, helping to **reduce severe illness and mortality worldwide**. In addition, **long-term medical research** has **improved the treatment of serious diseases** and **increased life expectancy**. Advances in areas such as cancer therapy and organ transplantation have **enabled many patients to live longer and more fulfilling lives**. **For these reasons**, continued investment in health research **remains indispensable**.

**On the other hand**, other fields may have **an even broader impact** because they **address problems that affect several aspects of human life at once**. **Environmental research**, for instance, is **vital for tackling climate change, pollution and declining food security**. **If these problems are not addressed**, they can **threaten agriculture, water supplies, ecosystems and public health simultaneously**. **Technological research** can also **generate benefits across multiple sectors**. Advances in **artificial intelligence, energy storage and communications** can **improve healthcare, transport, education and disaster response**, meaning that **progress in one field can support development in many others**.

**In conclusion**, health research should remain **a major scientific priority** because of its **direct contribution to human survival and well-being**. However, I believe research funding should not be **concentrated mainly on medicine**, since environmental and technological advances can **address wider and more interconnected challenges affecting society as a whole**.
