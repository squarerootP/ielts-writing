# OP3: charitable organizations and who they should support

---

**Some people think that charity organizations should only offer help to people of their own country. But others believe that these organizations should give aid to people in great need wherever they live. Discuss both views and give your opinion.**

---

## Model answer

Opinions differ over whether charities should concentrate on helping people within their own countries or provide assistance wherever the need is greatest. In my view, the most appropriate approach depends largely on the size and capacity of the organization.

On the one hand, smaller charities often have good reasons to focus on local communities. Their financial and human resources are usually limited, so operating domestically allows them to use these resources more efficiently. Staff can visit project sites regularly, monitor how funds are being used and respond quickly when problems arise, without incurring the high costs of international operations. In addition, local charities often have a better understanding of the needs of nearby communities. Because they interact closely with local residents, they can identify specific problems and provide support that is more accurately targeted.

On the other hand, larger charities are often well placed to work internationally. Their greater financial resources, professional staff and established networks enable them to assist vulnerable communities in countries where local support may be insufficient. This is particularly important during major humanitarian crises such as earthquakes, famines or disease outbreaks, when local institutions may be overwhelmed. International charities can also coordinate across borders, mobilize emergency supplies and direct assistance to areas where it is most urgently required.

In conclusion, charities should not all be expected to operate in the same way. Smaller organizations are often more effective when they concentrate on local needs, whereas larger charities should use their broader resources and networks to support people facing severe hardship, regardless of where they live.
